Submit a request for Temperature Data

This form is for submitting request on temperatures based on your area in Phoenix. This is for data collection where we are analyzing if temperatures are within expected ranges which in turn leads to better understanding of urban heat effect and climate change.

Purpose and Expectations

This form is voluntary. If you choose to participate, you will be asked to provide your email address, ZIP code, and details about your request. You may also provide your name and measurement details if you have taken a temperature reading (optional). The audience for this form is Phoenix residents who want to share or request neighborhood temperature data. The sections that are marked as (required) must be completed. If you have any issues completing this form please contact helpsupport@gmail.com. The result of this form will be used for presentation and aggregation purposes. If you ever feel you were put at risk due to this form, please contact the chair of the human subjects board. This form will take about 3-7 minutes to complete. After this is completed, the data will be stored in an excel sheet where we will use the data as evidence if your area is experiencing urban heat island effects.

Before you start

This form takes about 3 to 7 minutes. Fields marked (required) must be completed. All other fields are optional.

  • Email address (required): required format name@example.com
  • ZIP code (required): enter 5 digits, for example 85281.
  • Your request (required): choose one option.
  • Details (required): up to 240 characters.
  • Consent (required): check the box to confirm how your responses will be used.
  • Name and measurement details (optional): skip these if you did not take a temperature reading.
